About GeoCAD Tools

A free bilingual library of practical CAD, GIS and surveying scripts. Each tool includes the full source, usage steps, limitations and a relevant technical reference.

Additional Python tools run locally on your own files. The legacy AutoLISP and PyQGIS samples have not been tested inside their target applications; their limitations remain visible on their pages.

This project is independent and not affiliated with the products discussed.
